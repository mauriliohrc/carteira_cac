## Notes (colar no campo)
```
Carteira CAC ("CAC Wallet") is a PERSONAL DOCUMENT ORGANIZER for Brazilian sport shooters,
hunters and collectors (legally registered as "CAC" under Brazilian Army regulation).

It helps the user keep their own regulatory documents in order: firearm registration cards
(CRAF), transport permits (Guia de Tráfego), technical/psychological reports (Laudos) and the
collector's certificate (CR). The app tracks expiration dates and reminds the user before each
document expires.

IMPORTANT — what this app is NOT:
- It does NOT sell, buy, trade or facilitate the acquisition of firearms, ammunition or any weapon.
- It has NO marketplace, NO store, NO links to purchase weapons.
- It does NOT display violence or firearms in use.
- It is simply a private organizer for the user's OWN legal documents, like a digital folder.

PRIVACY / DATA:
- No account, no login, no server. Everything is stored locally on the device.
- The app does not collect any data.

HOW TO TEST PREMIUM (no purchase needed):
- Go to the "Mais" tab → "Código promocional" and enter:  Rocambole do Dino
- This unlocks the unlimited Premium tier for review (the free tier allows 1 firearm).
- Alternatively, the paywall ("Assinar Premium") works with a StoreKit sandbox account.

SECURITY:
- Optional app lock with a 6-digit PIN and Face ID unlock (Settings → "Mais" → Bloqueio do app).

The app is fully in Portuguese (Brazil), the target market.
```
